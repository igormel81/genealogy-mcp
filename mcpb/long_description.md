Turns Claude into a careful genealogist.

- **Where to look:** 79 archives and databases for Russia and the former USSR, Ukraine, Belarus, Poland, the Baltics, Western and Northern Europe, the British Isles, the USA and Latin America; which archive holds the records of a historical province today.
- **How to search:** name variants, pre-1918 spelling, male and female surname forms, 9 transliterations, Daitch–Mokotoff codes; live search in the Internet Archive full text, WikiTree, Open Archives and Wikidata.
- **Dates:** Julian ↔ Gregorian, church feasts, the Hebrew and French Republican calendars, Church Slavonic numerals, birth years from ages.
- **Is it our person?** Evidence checks, kinship names in Russian, English and Romanian, DNA (shared cM).
- **A project that remembers:** people, sources with full citations, a search log with negative results, GEDCOM import and export — kept in a folder on your computer.

Nothing is sent anywhere except the query of an online search you ask for, to that one site.
